## About 0xCARNEMUERTA

I’m a data and ML engineer moving deeper into cybersecurity. My interests include AI security, penetration testing, cloud security, threat intelligence, and threat research.

I especially enjoy understanding the full attack chain—from reconnaissance and initial access through exploitation, privilege escalation, and lateral movement. This site is where I document what I’m learning, testing, and building.

I’m interested in how systems fail, how attackers think, and how better security controls can be built from those lessons.

> Exploiting data, securing AI, and living between the tokens and the dead prompts.
